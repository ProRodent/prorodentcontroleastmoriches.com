Before you call, hold the broom.

  • Dry crumbly debris stays where it lies until dampened, as CDC advice discourages brushing or vacuuming it.

  • Count every soiled surface as contaminated, and keep gloved fingers away from your eyes and mouth.
  • Spray plenty, back away, wait a few minutes, then lift everything with disposable towels.
  • Knot the bag before you carry it, and walk it directly to the outdoor trash.
