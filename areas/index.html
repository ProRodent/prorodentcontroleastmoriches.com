What the type column tells you.

Suffolk County mixes two kinds of place. Hamlets lack a government of their own and sit inside a town, while villages are incorporated. A few places straddle two towns, and one or two share a name with the town around them, so the type column does real work when you call about a rodent in your house.

Range and ZIP queries.

Why isn't East Moriches a row in the table?

The coverage file we use names the 21 places shown and has no separate row for East Moriches' own ZIP code. Hamlet addresses are booked by street, so state yours and the visit gets confirmed on the call.

Where are Holtsville and Blue Point?

Their ZIP areas sit just beyond the fifteen-mile line, near 15.1 and 15.3 miles out. Phone to ask whether a special trip can be arranged.

My town is on the list, but I live at its edge. Is that a problem?

Not at all. A ZIP code that appears here is covered. Because distance is measured from the heart of each ZIP area, addresses at the edge get judged on the phone.

Is the Pine Street address an office?
