Hamlet, village or town: does it matter?

The label tells you who to ask about permits. It tells a technician nothing about your walls.

Calling a place a hamlet does not make its houses different from a village's. What differs is the lot, the age of the building and the way it is used.

The off-season problem.

Winter does not stop rodents. It sends them indoors, where they nest in insulation, stored boxes and quiet corners. A house that is closed for the season gives them an undisturbed stay.

Shoreline conditions to keep in mind.

Rust is a quiet problem near salt air. A vent screen that looks fine from the ground may have corroded at the edges, leaving a gap the size of a pencil. Coated or stainless mesh lasts longer.

Properties close to the water sometimes collect driftwood, seaweed and tangled branches after a storm. Heaps against siding hide gaps and give animals cover. Clear them promptly, and look for fresh burrows once the water recedes.
