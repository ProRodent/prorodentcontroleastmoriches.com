Before you call: five habits that keep dust down.

You can follow it without ever calling us; agencies hand the very same advice to every household that finds droppings indoors.

  • Your job is to dampen the mess first and move it second, so wetting always comes before sweeping.
  • Ventilation takes about thirty minutes, and it should happen before any cleaning starts.
  • Fingers wander to faces, so leave the gloves on until the final bag is knotted.
  • Spray plenty, back away, wait a few minutes, then lift everything with disposable towels.
  • Drop soiled towels and any dead animal into one plastic bag, using gloves or tongs, then tie it and place it in the household trash.
