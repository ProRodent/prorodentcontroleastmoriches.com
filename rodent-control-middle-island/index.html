Before you call: five habits that keep dust down.

Read every line once before you pick anything up, because a few calm minutes protect your lungs.

  • Leave the broom and the shop vacuum in the closet, because dry droppings crumble and the dust goes straight into your lungs.
  • Ventilation takes about thirty minutes, and it should happen before any cleaning starts.
  • Fingers wander to faces, so leave the gloves on until the final bag is knotted.
  • Spray plenty, back away, wait a few minutes, then lift everything with disposable towels.
  • A sealed bag stops stray particles from trailing through the rooms you cross.
  • Keep tabs on your health across the next few weeks, and see a physician should you develop flu-like illness or labored breathing.
