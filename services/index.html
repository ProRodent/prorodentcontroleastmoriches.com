How the pieces connect.

A typical sequence runs like this. The inspection names the animal and puts numbers on the work. Next, trapping clears whatever is inside, and sealing shuts the entries. If the house needs it, cleanup or repair closes the job.

Nothing forces you to buy the whole sequence. If you only need a vent cover swapped or a smell tracked down, say so, and the estimate lists that task alone.

About the service list.

Where is the emergency page?

There isn't one, deliberately. Urgent help is part of every page, someone answers the phone at all hours, and same-day visits go out when the calendar has room.

Where should I begin?

When you're unsure, start with an inspection. It names the animal and lays out the jobs in order, each with its own price.

Can I buy a single piece?

You can. Sealing, trapping, cleanup and repair are each bookable alone, although sealing ahead of repair saves doing the repair twice.

Are businesses welcome?

Yes. The commercial page covers how loading doors, shared walls and dumpsters get handled, along with what goes in the short report after each stop.
