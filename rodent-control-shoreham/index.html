Before you call: how the CDC says to clean up.

The list below paraphrases public health guidance and holds up whether or not you ever book a visit.

  • Your job is to dampen the mess first and move it second, so wetting always comes before sweeping.
  • Sheds, attics, garages and spare rooms hold stale air when shut, so crack them open and give them half an hour.
  • Fingers wander to faces, so leave the gloves on until the final bag is knotted.
  • Paper towels, not dry rags, pick up the wet waste, and you keep spraying until nothing looks powdery.
  • Ordinary garbage service takes the bag, and any reusable tool you used gets rinsed.
  • Mention the rodent contact to your doctor if illness appears within a few weeks.
