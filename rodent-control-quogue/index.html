Opening up a house after a long shut-in.

When a house has been closed for months, check before you unpack. Look at the attic hatch, the kitchen cabinets and the garage corners for pellets, shredded paper or a chewed corner of a box. Finding evidence early costs far less than finding a nest.

Closing up has a checklist too. Empty the cupboards of open food, seal pet food in tubs, set the garage door seal and ask someone to look in monthly. None of this removes a mouse already inside, but it stops the house from feeding new ones.

Clues that start a call.

Between the call and the visit.

Before you call: a safer way to tidy droppings.

  • Liquid binds the dust, which is why soaking always precedes wiping.
