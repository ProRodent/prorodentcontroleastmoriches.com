Before you call, read through these cleanup rules.

The list below paraphrases public health guidance and holds up whether or not you ever book a visit.

  • Using a leaf blower in a garage with droppings is the worst choice, and an ordinary vacuum cleaner comes second.
  • Open every opening in the room, then do something else for thirty minutes before you return.
  • Wash your hands thoroughly once the work ends, even when gloves stayed on throughout.
  • Soak pellets and urine marks in disinfectant or diluted household bleach and leave the liquid alone for about five minutes.
  • Add a second bag when the first is thin or heavy.
  • Linking symptoms to a cleanup is a job for a health professional, so seek one if you feel unwell.
